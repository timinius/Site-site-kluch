# Surface brief: index.html (+ portfolio.html, privacy.html, 404.html)

Scope: the studio's home landing page; portfolio.html is its companion gallery (Experience mode) inside the same world.
Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience and job: Yekaterinburg experts and small-business owners comparing studios; they need offer, price, term, proof and a working way to ask.
Action: submit the request form (name, phone or Telegram, site type, optional note). Every price row preselects its site type in the form.
Proof on hand: 27 projects, rating 4.9, 5+ years, two real reviews, 29 portfolio collages of commercial graphic design.
Constraints: static hosting (GitHub Pages / Netlify); form via Web3Forms (Netlify Forms and PHP as switchable options); no fabricated contacts, cases or claims.
Build path: code-led. The owner delegated every decision and is unavailable, so comp-led's human plate review cannot run; ambition lives in this contract and the signature interaction.
Unresolved: contact details, Web3Forms access key, privacy-policy operator data; old copy's 21-day process vs 14-day promise and 15 min vs 2 h reply (redesign uses 14 days and 15 minutes; flagged to owner).

## Direction contract

THESIS: "Сайт под ключ" made literal. The page is a malachite door locked by a copper key; scrolling turns the key and the door opens onto the studio's work. It refuses the category default: a dark neon agency hero with a browser mockup, a row of service cards and three pricing cards.

OWN-WORLD: An Ural workshop. Polished banded malachite is the ground of the opening and the close; the works wall sits on deep green-black; reading sections sit on pale mineral white tinted toward malachite; copper carries every action, rule and highlight. Sofia Sans Extra Condensed set big and uppercase like a constructivist poster, Sofia Sans for text. Services are a printed price list (прейскурант) with dotted leaders and copper-and-malachite objects; the process is a 14-day work schedule.

STORY: The visitor reads the offer and sees the action at once, turns the key by scrolling, meets a wall of real work, reads one price list that answers type, price, term and inclusions, sees the 14-day schedule, reads real reviews and numbers, and sends a request that actually arrives.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-viewport malachite door split by a vertical seam near 60% width. Top: wordmark left, nav and a copper "Обсудить проект" right. Left leaf: H1 in three uppercase condensed lines at display max ("Сайт / под ключ / за 14 дней"), an 18-20px subline with price and terms, a copper primary button and a "Смотреть работы" link. Right leaf: the copper key, about 45vw wide, lying horizontally with its bit entering the seam.

FORM: Owner-pinned direction "Уральская мастерская" (no concept-seed roll). Signature interaction: scroll-linked key turn, then the two leaves part to reveal the works wall; reduced motion gets a static door and normal flow.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
