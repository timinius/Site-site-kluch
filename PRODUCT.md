# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step (existing codebase). Hosting: GitHub Pages or Netlify (user answer), so there is no server code in production; lead delivery goes through a static-friendly form service.

## Users

Experts (photographers, designers, coaches, psychologists) and small-business owners in Yekaterinburg (shops, services; one confirmed client runs an auto-parts store) who need a website and are comparing several studios. They arrive from search or ads, often on a phone, skeptical and price-sensitive. Their job: find out what they will get, for how much, by when, and whether this studio can be trusted, then leave a request.

## Product Purpose

WEB.EKB is a small studio that designs, builds and launches websites "под ключ" (turnkey): portfolio sites, online stores, landing pages and corporate sites. The site exists to turn a visiting business owner into a lead (a submitted request) and to make the offer feel concrete and safe. Success: more submitted requests from qualified local businesses.

## Positioning

Fixed, published prices (from 30 000 ₽), a 14-day turnaround, design + development + launch in one package with no hidden extras, a contract with 50% prepayment, training on how to run the site, and support after launch. The studio's visual muscle is proven by a large body of commercial graphic design (ad creatives, Instagram stories, YouTube thumbnails, banners, logos) for real brands.

## Operating Context

A visitor typically compares 3-5 studio sites, looks at prices and examples, and contacts the one that feels most certain. Contact happens by phone or Telegram after a short free call where the studio shows examples for the visitor's niche.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services and prices: Портфолио / Лендинг 30 000 ₽ (3 revisions, 14 days); Корпоративный сайт 55 000 ₽ (multi-page, SEO + analytics, 5 revisions, 14 days); Интернет-магазин 75 000 ₽ (catalog with filters, cart and online payment, SEO + analytics, team training, 1 month support; term not stated).
- Process: request and free call → choosing site type → design with approvals → development and testing (responsive + SEO included) → launch and training, support after launch.
- Lead form: name, phone or Telegram, site type. Must actually deliver leads (previous version silently dropped them).
- Open decisions: contact details (phone / Telegram / email) were never provided; the form-service access key must be added by the owner; the privacy-policy operator details are unknown. The old copy promised both "14 days" and a 21-day process timeline, and both "reply in 15 minutes" and "within 2 hours"; the redesign uses 14 days and 15 minutes and flags this for the owner.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: WEB.EKB, based in Yekaterinburg. The old logo and look are discarded by the owner's request.
- The owner chose the "Ural workshop" direction for the redesign: the turnkey key as the central image, Ural malachite and copper as materials, typography in the spirit of Yekaterinburg constructivism.

## Evidence on Hand

- Confirmed real by the owner: 27 delivered projects, average rating 4.9, 5+ years on the market.
- Two real testimonials: Анна Фёдорова (photographer, Yekaterinburg), Дмитрий Казаков (auto-parts store owner).
- Portfolio: 29 collage images of commercial graphic design in `img/works/` (converted from `img2/`).
- Absent, must not be fabricated: website case studies with screenshots, team or founder photos, client logos, legal entity details, additional reviews or numbers.

## Product Principles

1. Certainty over hype: price, term and what is included are visible before the visitor has to ask.
2. Show the work: the portfolio is the proof, so it gets real estate, not a link in the footer.
3. Every path ends at a working request form that really delivers.
4. Local and specific: this is a Yekaterinburg workshop, not a faceless agency.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No specific requirement was stated; baseline is WCAG 2.2 AA (inferred): readable contrast, full keyboard use, reduced-motion support, mobile-first.
